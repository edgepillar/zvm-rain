    How a rain pays

    1. People talk in a group the bot is in. It remembers, per group, who spoke when.
    2. You send /rain 100, /rain 50 wqsr or /rain 100 5 (the last five minutes).
    3. The bot splits the amount evenly between everyone linked who spoke in that group in that window, never you, and the Rain contract pays them out of what you approved.

    The contract refuses to spend from anyone who has not linked, to pay anyone who has not linked, to pay one address twice, or to pay one rain twice. Calling unlink stops everything at once.
